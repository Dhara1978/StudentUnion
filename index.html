Who Are We?

We are the Student Union, representing the voices and interests of all students at our university.

We are an independent organisation run by students for students.

We act as a voice for students and work to improve their academic and social experience.

We run and fund various activities and initiatives to enhance the student experience.

We can offer support and advice on a wide range of issues affecting student life.
